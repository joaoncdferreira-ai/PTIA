Never give up on your dreams. 🚀🚀🚀

I had the privilege of attending #GoogleIO2026 in Mountain View, California. An experience that became an important milestone in my journey in technology.
What made this opportunity especially meaningful is that Google I/O is an invitation-only event designed for developers. My path there began almost a year ago when I joined Google's Women Techmakers community. Since then, I've participated in Google programs, training sessions, and community initiatives that have continuously helped me grow both personally and professionally.
In April, I received the invitation. It was one of those moments that felt surreal because attending Google I/O had always been a dream of mine.
From the moment I arrived, I was inspired by the incredible diversity of the developer community. I met talented professionals from France, India, China, Portugal, Greece, Brazil, the United Kingdom, and many other parts of the world. It was a powerful reminder that technology has the ability to connect people across cultures, backgrounds, and experiences.
Walking into Shoreline Amphitheatre for the keynote was unforgettable. Hearing directly from leaders such as Sundar Pichai, Josh Woodward, Demis Hassabis, Liz Reid, Vidhya Srinivasan, and many others provided a front-row view into how AI is reshaping the future of technology.
One of the strongest themes throughout the event was the rise of agentic AI. Since my primary interests are AI and Cloud, I especially enjoyed sessions such as:
• Defining the Agentic AI Era
• Build Agents with Gemini API
• Supercharge Your AI Coding Workflow with Chrome DevTools for Agents
• Build Intelligent Android Apps with Google's AI
• The Future of Software Development

Beyond the sessions, I had the opportunity to explore hands-on demos such as Google Beam, XREAL Project Aura, Android XR experiences, and the AI Sandbox. I also experienced my first ride in a Waymo, which felt like stepping into the future.
But more than the technology, what I'll remember most is the community, the people building, teaching, sharing, and collaborating to create technology that can positively impact lives around the world.
A year ago, I joined Women Techmakers seeking mentorship, learning opportunities, and a stronger connection to the technology community. This year, I found myself at Google Headquarters surrounded by some of the brightest minds in technology, exchanging ideas, learning from experts, and building new connections.

I'm returning to New York inspired, grateful, and excited about what's ahead in AI, Cloud, and the developer ecosystem.

Thank you to Women Techmakers, Google Developer Groups (GDG), Google, and everyone who makes the event possible.
See you at Google I/O 2027.
